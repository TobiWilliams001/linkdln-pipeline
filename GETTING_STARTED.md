# First-Time Use Guide

## 1. Sign in
Go to the site and click **Get started**.
Enter your email → check your inbox → click the link. This automatically creates your workspace — no invite needed.

## 2. Set up your profile (one-time)
The first time you sign in, you'll land on a short setup form:
- Your name or brand
- Voice profile — how you actually talk (type it, or upload a recording and it'll transcribe automatically)
- Core belief, your ideal client's problem, origin story
- A few best results (one per line: `situation | outcome`)
- Strong opinions (one per line)
- How many posts per week you want, and how often you'll check in (weekly, every two weeks, or monthly)

Save → you're in.

## 3. The weekly (or biweekly/monthly) loop
1. **This week's input** — answer four short prompts (~10 min). The first one can be a voice note instead of typing.
2. Submit → drafts generate automatically and you're taken to **Drafts**.
3. Read each draft, edit if you want, then **Approve**.
4. Once approved, **Mark as posted** (optionally paste the live LinkedIn link).
5. **Dashboard** shows your monthly count: generated / approved / posted.

You can revisit **Profile** anytime from the sidebar to update your voice, results, or check-in cadence.

## Notes
- Open signup — anyone with an email can create a workspace by signing in.
- Reminders respect your chosen check-in frequency, not just weekly.
- Only one input per week; resubmitting the same week edits it rather than generating a second batch of drafts.
